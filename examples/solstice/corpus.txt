Solstice-Preview is a compact image understanding and text generation research model.
The visual fabric divides images into local patches and groups similar patches into regions.
The language fabric predicts tokens from hierarchical contexts at several time scales.
Sparse episodic conditioning helps the generator reuse relevant learned responses.
Tool calls are typed, schema validated, budgeted, and executed outside the language generator.
A CPU stores the full fabric while an optional GPU accelerates active visual comparisons and local updates.
A six gigabyte GPU is treated as a working-set accelerator rather than the sole model store.
The model should report uncertainty when its training evidence is weak.
